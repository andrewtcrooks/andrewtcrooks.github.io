What you get

A spreadsheet: the current file the day your invoice is paid, then a refreshed file every Monday through January 31. One row per contractor: company, owner, phone, city, trade, years licensed, current carrier, current agency, liability limit, policy expiration and days left. Two more tabs list contractors licensed in the last 14 days and policy cancellations filed with L&I. Filter by trade or city in the sheet.

Where it comes from

Washington State Department of Labor & Industries contractor registration records, published as public-domain open data and refreshed daily. Contractors must keep liability insurance and a bond on file to stay registered, so the state records every carrier and every expiration date. We filter, join and schedule it. Nothing is scraped and no consumer data is involved.

Why renewal dates

A contractor takes a competing quote in the 60 to 90 days before renewal and almost never otherwise. In Skagit County alone, 247 contractor policies renew in the next 90 days (state data, November 11, 2026). 46 of those contractors list no agency at all: they bought online or direct.
