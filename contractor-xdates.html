What you get

A spreadsheet by 7 AM Monday. One row per contractor: company, owner, phone, city, trade, years licensed, current carrier, current agency, liability limit, policy expiration and days left. A second tab lists contractors licensed in the last seven days. Filter by trade or city in the sheet.

Where it comes from

Washington State Department of Labor & Industries contractor registration records, published as public-domain open data and refreshed daily. Contractors must keep liability insurance and a bond on file to stay registered, so the state records every carrier and every expiration date. We filter, join and schedule it. Nothing is scraped and no consumer data is involved.

Why renewal dates

A contractor takes a competing quote in the 60 to 90 days before renewal and almost never otherwise. In Skagit County alone, 263 contractor policies renew in the next 90 days. About one in five of those contractors lists no agency at all: they bought online from Next, Hiscox, biBERK or Thimble and have no local agent.

Your own clients

Tell us your agency name as it appears on your clients' L&I filings. We remove that book before every send, so every row is a prospect.
