What happens when someone calls after hours

1

It answers

Calls forward to the receptionist after hours or when nobody picks up. It greets callers as your company and says the call is recorded.

2

It gets the details

Name, address, callback number and what the system is doing, read back to confirm. A gas smell gets "leave the house and call 911" before anything else.

3

It books the job

It offers the earliest open window from your real calendar, confirms it, and puts the appointment on your schedule with everything the caller said.

4

You get the summary

An email within a minute: who called, from where, what's wrong, whether it's an emergency, what got booked. Recording and transcript kept.
