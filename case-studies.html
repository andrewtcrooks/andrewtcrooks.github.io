Private document intelligence

Turning a private document set into searchable answers

The problem

A confidential matter kept generating scanned and digital documents: letters, records, filings and correspondence. Finding a fact meant opening files one by one, and the documents were too sensitive to upload to a cloud AI service.

What we built

  • Automatic intake: every new document is captured, OCR'd and filed
  • Full-text and meaning-based search across the whole set
  • AI analysis that classifies documents and extracts dates, parties and key facts
  • Everything runs on private infrastructure; nothing is shared with a third-party AI tool by default

Result

Finding a fact became a search instead of a read-through, and new documents are organized the moment they arrive instead of piling up.

Built with

Paperless-ngx · OCR · Qdrant vector search · LLM analysis · Docker

Self-hosted operations

Running a whole business on self-hosted, monitored infrastructure

The problem

A small business needs ERP, email, automation, analytics and monitoring, but per-seat SaaS bills add up, and every tool keeps its own copy of the company's data.

What we built

  • ERP (accounting, CRM, invoicing) connected to sales and banking feeds
  • Workflow automation, mail, web analytics and a vector database on one platform
  • Uptime monitoring with alerts, plus backups
  • Secure remote access without exposing services to the internet

Result

More than 50 monitored services run DataNova's own operations today. The same patterns are what we deploy for clients in a Self-Hosted Automation Stack.

Built with

ERPNext · n8n · PostgreSQL · Docker · Uptime Kuma · Mailcow · Tailscale · reverse proxy

Applied AI

Rook CEO: an AI operations platform that works a real business

The problem

A one-person consultancy cannot watch every job board, bid portal and inbox, keep a CRM current, and still deliver client work.

What we built

  • AI agents that scan job boards and public bid portals daily and qualify each lead
  • Draft proposals and social posts that the founder reviews and approves before anything goes out
  • Every qualified lead recorded in the ERP with notes and follow-up reminders
  • Model routing, usage limits and an automatic budget guard so AI costs stay predictable

Result

Lead research, follow-ups and reporting run on their own every day; a person approves anything that leaves the building. It is DataNova's own product, and it runs DataNova's sales pipeline.

Built with

LLM agents · workflow scheduling · ERPNext API · Discord approvals · usage monitoring
